Yes, Minister is a favorite of mine, so this has been on my radar for some time, but my Mormon sensibilities kept me from watching such a sweary show for a long time. (To be fair, I don't know if I knew how to stream it either).

It is a delightful mashup of the sweary incompetence of Slow Horses with the premise of Yes, Minister, and I'm glad I watched it even if it didn't always land with me.
