Forgot to mention this in the *Walkaway* review I just posted, but while "Claude might be alive" types have ruined a lot of "is AI human?" stories for me, Doctorow dodged that entirely. Instead of asking how human mechanistic computation is,
he asks how mechanistic and computational humans are, and that's a much more productive approach.
