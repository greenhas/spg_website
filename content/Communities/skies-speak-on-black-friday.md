I recently began [a project](https://spencergreenhalgh.com/myself/reviewblogging-my-way-through-my-music-collection/) of reviewblogging my way through my music collection, and the indie electronic musician [Skies Speak](https://spencergreenhalgh.com/artists/skies-speak/) has been an early focus.

I discovered Skies Speak earlier this year, thanks to the Farmhand comic series (which he put together a soundtrack for), and I've enjoyed most of his music. I'm less interested in his *At War With the World* album, but there's an exception to that exception, and it's the track "Black Friday (Veni, Veni, Emmanuel)":

{{<youtube id="15Agu-1prZM">}}

I love this Advent hymn and I hate Black Friday, and their combination here feels powerful. If the original hymn draws from the pain and longing of the Babylonian exile, Skies Speak's take on the hymn forces us to ask if we have only exiled ourselves, distancing ourself from God's preferred present by building abominations like Black Friday.
