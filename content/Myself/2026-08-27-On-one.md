On one hand, this is the first manga I've read where I saw why the art form is as revered as it is. Good art, interesting premise (somewhat silly, but no more than XIII, which I love more than I should), and not too gratuitous (but still some).

On the other, I felt that it didn't catch my attention for the whole volume, and I'm torn on whether I'd enjoy reading the next four.
