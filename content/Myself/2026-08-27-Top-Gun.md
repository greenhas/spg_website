Top Gun is probably the better movie, but if I had to watch one US Navy aviation recruitment video dressed up as a blockbuster film, I think I'd pick this one. It wrestles with more interesting ideas, and more of it happens on a carrier, which is fun.

And yet, I am annoyed by two things that I recognize stand in tension with each other: 1) the jingoistic "look how cool our war toys are" vibes and 2) the shaggy dog nature of the story that ultimately rules out showing off those war toys and doesn't do anything with the time travel premise that a short story couldn't do better.
