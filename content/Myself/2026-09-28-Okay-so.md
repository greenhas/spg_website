Okay, so I didn't actually read the final TPB. I cobbled together individual issues from hoopla (boooo, hoopla) but couldn't be bothered to review them individually.

That's probably good, because I didn't like the first issue at all—I had trouble remembering what had happened in the previous TPB and struggled to get back into the plot. I still think the plot is kind of dumb, but reading the remaining issues reminds me that I like Guillory's characters, art, and clear personal investment in this story. I'm being generous with my rating, but I think he's earned it. (Chew is still too weird for me to try, though).
