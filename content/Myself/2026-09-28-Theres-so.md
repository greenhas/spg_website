There's so much about this series that shouldn't work. The protagonist is clearly meant to be feminist but borders on angry manhater and is clad in typical comic book superheroine attire. Would it surprise you to know the creative team is all male?

Yet, for all of its very clear faults, there's something about it that works? Or at least that I found to be an engaging read. Maybe there's something here under the problems, or maybe I'm just part of the problem. 🤷🏼‍♂️
