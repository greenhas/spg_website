There are things I could critique about this series, and the last story in this omnibus feels like an awkward not-quite-but-maybe-finale that makes me worried about what's to come, but it's so darn cozy that it's hard to find fault with it. I love a story that combines the fantastic with the mundane, and this does it so, so well.
