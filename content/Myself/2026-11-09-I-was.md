I was generous in my review for Season 3 because I was carrying a lot of good will forward. I enjoyed parts of Season 4, but I spent the first few episodes asking myself if the show had always been that zany (probably) and the next few wondering if it was doing all its characters justice. I don't regret having watched it, and a lot of it was fun, but it wasn't the best season of the show.
