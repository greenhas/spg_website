When I first started making my way through this Image Humble Bundle about a year ago (SO many comics), I got really annoyed at all the comics that could be summed up as "let's get as edgy as possible so that people know comics are a mature medium."

This brought me right back to that. It was gross and dark, and the best reason I had for finishing the damn thing was that I write too many positive reviews, and I figured having a negative one would balance things out.
