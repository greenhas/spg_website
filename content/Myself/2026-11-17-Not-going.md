Not going to argue that this is some of the best music ever made in human history, but that's not what my albumblogging is about. My albumblogging is about what I like in music, and I like this.

I like TMBG to be weird but not distractingly weird. This album is wonderful working music: It easily sits in the background without pulling my attention from what I'm doing, but it also reminds me of my favorite TMBG songs and just how bizarre they are.ew
