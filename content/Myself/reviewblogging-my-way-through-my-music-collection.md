My last two full-length blog posts have been about [further reorienting my online presence around my website](https://spencergreenhalgh.com/myself/taking-a-break-from-mastodon-and-bluesky-but-not-microblogging/) and [finding a new music player that solves a complaint I've had for years](https://spencergreenhalgh.com/myself/enjoying-the-trove-music-player/), and this post is going to build on that momentum. Last night, while updating my Siri Shortcuts to work with some updates to my Hugo site (in short, a new theme and some new template setups that I'm happy with so far), I built a new Shortcut that would let me post a review of a music album that I've listened to.

I've been leaning more into album listening in recent years (when, of course, I'm not listening to internet radio), and the fact that my new music player does not do playlists very well is only further emphasizing that. It's also time to admit that I don't need to hold on to every single mp3 that I've collected over the years and do a little bit of purging of tracks that came preloaded on my first college laptop twenty years ago (oh, geez) and that I haven't listened to since. Going album by album through my music will give me a chance to explore more things I can do with my blog (not that reviewing an album is all that different than reviews I already do) and help me do some digital decluttering while I'm at it.
