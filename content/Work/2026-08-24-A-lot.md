A lot of what I was looking for in this book is already available in other sources, and while I appreciated the normalizing of Latter-day Saint temples for a lay audience, it would also smack of apologetics if Stapley weren't also so unflinching in addressing controversy and taboo. It's a wonderful resource, and it's accounting for important events of the last decade made it an invaluable read.
