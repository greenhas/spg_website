I'm not a lawyer or a tech policy expert, but I can't say I'm reassured by the talk of misconceptions here. My reading of the regulations is that they are so broad as to be able to cover the use of personal devices on a university network, for example, whether or not they are intended to cover that.

I'm reminded of signing an apartment contract, being worried about a clause that allows someone to enter at any time, and being reassured that the clause won't actually ever be used. Whatever the landlord reassures me about actual intent, the language in the contract goes much broader than that. If the intent is narrower, the language ought to be, too.
