Hello! My name is Spencer Greenhalgh, and this website is the core of my online presence. To combat [context collapse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Context_collapse), I've divided this website into multiple sections. Chances are that most visitors to this website are mainly interested in one of those sections!

You can find posts and pages about my personal interests and projects at [Myself](/myself), about family life and friendships at [Relationships](/Relationships), about my teaching and research at [Work](/work), and about politics and religion at [Communities](/communities).

I also blog in a number of formats that cross-cut these four categories: [long-form blog posts](/types/macro), [microblog posts](/types/macro), [microblog posts with pictures](/types/photo), [responses to news articles and podcasts](/types/link), [media reviews](/types/media), and [tea reviews](/types/tea/).  You can read about how to better navigate reviews on [this page](/reviews).

If you want to get really fine-grained in seeing what I write about, check out my [Tags](/tags) page and click on a tag that you may be interested in.
